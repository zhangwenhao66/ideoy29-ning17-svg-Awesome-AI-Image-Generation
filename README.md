# 🎨 Awesome AI Image Generation & Editing Resources

A curated list of leading **AI image models** and **tools** for creators, designers, and developers.  
Explore state-of-the-art models for image generation, then discover useful online tools — including our own platform — to bring your ideas to life.

## 🧠 Models

- [Midjourney](https://www.midjourney.com/) — A generative AI program that converts natural-language prompts into highly realistic images.  
- [GPT-4o](https://openai.com/index/introducing-4o-image-generation/) — A multimodal model that natively supports image generation and editing along with text and image input.  
- [FLUX.1 Kontext](#) — A flow-matching AI model family for unified text-to-image and image-to-image editing, optimized for iterative, context-aware modifications.  
- [Nano Banana](#) — An advanced image-editor model known for ultra-fast results, strong character consistency and natural-language image edits.  

## 🛠️ Tools

- [Ideoy](https://ideoy.ai) - Remake this week's viral meme videos from one selfie (AI image-to-video, 9:16, freemium).
- [NanoEditor](https://nanoeditor.app) — Free AI image generator and editor supporting text-to-image, image-to-image, and online editing.
- [Pixlr](https://pixlr.com/) — Modern AI-powered online photo editor offering background removal, transparent images and one-click smart edits.
- [Fotor](https://www.fotor.com/) — Free online AI photo editor for text commands, upscaling, style transfer and batch auto editing.
- [Canva](https://www.canva.com/ai-image-generator/) — AI Image Generator and photo editing suite enabling text-to-image, DALL·E-style generation and easy design workflows.
- [DeepAI Image Editor](https://deepai.org/machine-learning-model/image-editor) — Upload images or use a URL and edit them with AI via simple prompts in a free web tool.
- [PicWish](https://picwish.com/) — Online AI photo editor focused on removing backgrounds, unblurring images and enhancing product-style visuals with ease.
